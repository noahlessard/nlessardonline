Ok, now onto the cool stuff. I got to go the <Link href="https://pimaair.org/">Pima Air and Space Museum</Link> last month! Arizona (really deserts in general) are great places for <Link href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_graveyard">aircraft boneyards</Link>, and this museum had a great collection thanks to that fact. 
There was also an unique presentation format; instead of planes being done up in hangers, most of them were just outside. 

I liked this because it was kind of like an airshow, where you can look across the lot and see all the planes you want to visit, then walk over.
And on the walk over you get to pass lots of other interesting planes that you can stop and look at, get up close to, get lots of good camera angles.
I took a lot of pictures, lots more than I'll put here, but these were the best ones I took / saw. Unfortunately, the <Link href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convair_B-36_Peacemaker">B-36</Link> that I was excited to see was under maintenance, so I'll be back someday to see that for sure.
